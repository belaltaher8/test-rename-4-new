# A Poem of Code and Dreams

In the quiet hum of midnight's glow,
Where lines of code like rivers flow,
A programmer sits, both tired and keen,
Before a bright computer screen.

Each function called, each loop that spins,
A world of logic that begins,
With variables that dance and play,
Through algorithms night and day.

The bugs may hide in shadows deep,
While deadlines loom and steal our sleep,
But still we code with hearts of flame,
For creation is our truest aim.

In zeros, ones, a story told,
Of problems solved and futures bold,
Where every commit, both big and small,
Builds something meaningful for all.

So here's to those who dream in code,
Who walk the less-traveled road,
Your passion lights the digital sky,
And makes the impossible comply.
